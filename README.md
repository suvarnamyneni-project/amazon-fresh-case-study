# Shop by Recipe for Amazon Fresh — UX case study

Case study and clickable prototype by **Suvarna Myneni**, UX Designer at Amazon Development Centre India (August 2020 – February 2023). She led Shop by Recipe from concept to launch.

- Case study: https://suvarnamyneni.github.io/amazon-fresh-case-study/
- Prototype: https://suvarnamyneni.github.io/amazon-fresh-case-study/walkthrough.html

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Shared layout and type live in `case-study.css`. Page-specific layout is in `index.html`. Final screens, research figures, competitor captures, and persona portraits are local WebP files in `img/` (not hotlinked). Hotspots are defined in `screens.js`.

The page includes the research and the design: competitor comparison, survey of 48 people, four personas (Ankit, Sakshi, Harsh, Meera), three end-user stories, user flow, information architecture, wireframes, seven high-fidelity screens, a UI kit sampled from those screens, and the clickable prototype.

Metrics on the page are labeled by source:

- Shop by Recipe after launch: +35% ingredient bundle sales, −20% cart abandonment, 90% positive feedback.
- Food and Fresh discovery redesign (not Shop by Recipe): +40% app usage and +25% average order value.
- Survey research: 48 people, 95.8%, 73%, 67%, 58.3%, and 60 / 50 healthy and veg meals.
- Recipe-to-cart conversion, basket completeness, repeat shopping, and time to build a cart are intended measures, not results.
